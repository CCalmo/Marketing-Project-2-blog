Don't Wait, Start now

Learning financial literacy is a pretty important skill when it comes to adulthood, but why wait for teens to reach that point before introducing them?

Letting teens budget at an early age is one of the best ways on how to teach financial literacy to students and teens. However, the problem lies in finding good budgeting apps that captivates teenagers.

Fortunately for us, SaveNShare is a personal finance app designed with teens and young adults in mind guiding them along the path to build these critical skills

Why pick SaveNShare?

SaveNShare is a completely free budgeting app with no hidden fees attached. Transparent about their work, what makes SaveNShare stand out compared to other teen budgeting apps, is that it allows user to split their earnings into buckets.

Unlike the top budget apps, SaveNShare builds the habit of always giving back. Being one of the unique apps that give back, SaveNShare matches charitable donations to that of the savings milestone achieved.

Whether you're a parent looking for a free savings app for kids to help introduce basic financial concepts, or a teacher searching for savings app for teens to engage with your students, SaveNShare combines both practical financial teachings with building the habit of always giving back
