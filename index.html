Why wait for adulthood?

Learning financial literacy is a pretty important skill when it comes to adulthood, but why wait for teens to reach that point before introducing them?

Letting teens budget at an early age is one of the best ways on how to teach financial literacy to students and teens. However, the problem lies in finding good budgeting apps that captivates teenagers.

Fortunately for us, SaveNShare is a personal finance app designed with teens and young adults in mind guiding them along the path to build these critical skills

Why pick SaveNShare?

SaveNShare is a completely free budgeting app with no hidden fees attached. transparent about their work, what makes it stand out compared to other teen budgeting apps, is that it allows user to split their earnings into buckets. Unlike the top budget apps, SaveNShare builds the habit of always giving back.
